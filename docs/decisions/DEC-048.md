title: >-
  Quality Management is the IQF in operation: one tab per standard a person is linked to by scope, submissions per item
  and period, an approval chain ending with the QA office and the champion
status: accepted
context: >-
  The owner (2026-11-23) defined how the system works: the IQF is set up and mapped in Settings; each standard is scoped
  to courses, programs, units or the institution; evidence is linked to standards. Quality Management is where
  coordinators, heads, deans and vice presidents meet the standards by submitting the linked evidence for their courses,
  programs and units on the standard's frequency, and where it is signed off. Example: a Course Learning Assessment
  standard asks each course coordinator, every semester in week 12, for the assessment of CLOs, the course and
  instructor evaluation, a reflection and the closing of the loop; the submission then goes to the program coordinator,
  the dean, the QA office and the champion (the VPAA).
decision: >-
  Quality Management shows exactly one tab per top-level IQF standard the person is linked to through scope: as the one
  who submits for a scoped item (the course's coordinator, the program's coordinator, the unit's head; for the
  institution, the champion), or as an approver in its chain. Administrators and members of the QA office see every
  standard read-only. Inside a tab the standard and its sub-standards are read-only, and the person sees one submission
  per scoped item and period. Frequency is set on a standard and inherited by its sub-standards (a sub-standard may
  override or opt out): daily; weekly on a weekday; monthly on a day or the nth or last weekday; each semester in week N
  of the term (from the academic calendar); yearly in a month on a day or the nth or last weekday. A submission is for
  the standard where the frequency is set, and asks for every evidence item linked to it and to the sub-standards that
  inherit its frequency: a document is uploaded or a version reused; a dataset is the version that holds the item's and
  period's rows. The approval chain is set on a standard and inherited: steps such as the program coordinator of the
  course's program or the head of the course's college, then always the QA office (the members and head of the unit
  named as the quality office in Settings) and last the champion. A step whose approver is the submitter is skipped; a
  step nobody can take is flagged, never guessed. Each step approves ("Internally approved") or returns with a note;
  return goes back to the submitter. A fully approved submission is frozen by the database. The old tabs (Standards,
  Cycles, Plans, Programs, Syllabi, My work) leave Quality Management.
rationale: >-
  The tabs mirror the IQF the person answers for, so each person sees only their work; the frequency and the chain are
  configuration on the standard (DEC-002), the evidence is the evidence list's (stored once, reused by accreditation
  through the IQF mapping).
consequences: >-
  Migration 0089: frequency and approval steps on requirements (by framework and code, like scope), course scope items,
  the quality office unit, submissions with their evidence and steps. The Course scope level lists courses. Settings ->
  IQF sets frequency and approval steps. Accreditation harvesting approved submissions and the fate of Reports come
  later.
