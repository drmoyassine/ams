title: >-
  General education and Free electives are institution defaults with program overrides; a study-plan course may count
  toward several concentrations
status: accepted
context: >-
  The approved AIU study plan (2026-11-23) asks different General education totals per program (36, 38 for engineering,
  39 for biotechnology), different area minimums per program (for example humanities 3 or 6), a Free electives block
  next to General education, and courses shared by several "choose one" concentrations (CVE481 in Smart Cities and BIM,
  CSC476 in AI and Data Science).
decision: >-
  The institution's catalog settings hold the default General education credits and Free elective credits; a program may
  set its own (program.gened_credits, program.free_elective_credits; empty means the default). Each General education
  area keeps its institutional minimum and a program may set its own minimum per area (program_gened_area; 0 means it
  does not ask for it). Free electives is its own requirement block, next to General education, not a GenEd area. A
  study-plan row may count toward several concentrations (course_program_concentration, shown as chips); none means
  every student of the program. Checks use the program's values when set, else the institution's.
rationale: >-
  Academic Affairs owns one General education design while programs differ in totals and minimums; overrides keep one
  source and make each exception visible. Free electives are not General education, so they are counted and printed
  separately. A shared concentration course is one row, not duplicates.
consequences: >-
  Migration 0087 (drops course_program.concentration_id after copying it). The workbook gains Program GenEd areas, the
  Programs columns GenEd credits and Free elective credits, the Catalog setting Free elective credits, and several
  concentration codes in a Study plan row separated by ;. Catalog snapshots carry each program's GenEd values.
