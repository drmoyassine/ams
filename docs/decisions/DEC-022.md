id: DEC-022
title: External standards drafted by analysis are gated by publishing, not by per-line approval
status: accepted
context: >-
  External standards are fixed by the accreditor. Per-line "reviewed/approved" labels were confusing and, on seeded
  data, meaningless.
decision: >-
  Analysis writes requirements into a draft version in the accreditor's exact wording, marked as AI-made with page and
  quote. Publishing the version is the review gate. A note may be "required" only with a verified quote (DEC-006).
  Proposals about the IQF (mappings, new IQF items) stay proposals until accepted. Re-running analysis never overwrites
  what a person edited.
rationale: The owner approved it; it keeps AI proposing and humans publishing without line-by-line ceremony.
consequences: Per-line reviewStatus on external requirements is retired in the UI when the compiler lands.
