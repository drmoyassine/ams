id: DEC-017
title: The AMS and the institutional data warehouse are separate products, each with its own storage
status: accepted
context: >-
  The institution is planning a separate AI-native BI product (source systems, warehouse on PostgreSQL and S3, a
  governed metric layer, consumers). The question was whether the AMS should keep its own evidence storage or read
  everything from the warehouse.
decision: >-
  The AMS and the warehouse are separate products. Each owns its storage: the AMS keeps its own database and its own
  S3-compatible store for evidence files and accreditation records. The AMS consumes data from the warehouse (governed
  metrics for datasets) through an optional adapter. Either product works without the other; when no warehouse is
  connected, datasets come in by manual upload.
rationale: >-
  Independent products can be deployed, sold and upgraded on their own. Accreditation records and evidence keep their
  own integrity rules in the AMS, and the warehouse keeps metric definitions and computation.
consequences: >-
  Confirms DEC-014 and PRD 9.5-9.6: the dataset source switch (manual upload or governed metric) stays, and the governed
  metric adapter is built against the warehouse API when it exists. Nothing already built moves.
