id: DEC-029
title: 'Quality Management runs the IQF; cadences live in the framework with unit overlays; one obligation scheduler and one action register serve Quality Management and Accreditations'
status: accepted
context: >-
  The owner added a Quality Management menu item above Accreditations (formerly Engagements) to operationalise the IQF,
  and asked for careful thought on how cycles are defined: at the level of the whole IQF, a standard or sub-standard, and
  evidence collection, and the same for external standards.
decision: >-
  1. Each unit or program runs its own rhythm; there is no shared institution year. 2. Two kinds of time: a review
  cadence on any framework node (inherited down the tree, overridable) and an evidence cadence on each evidence
  expectation of a requirement. 3. Default cadences are part of the framework version (versioned, proposable by the AI
  from the document, published by a person); each subject may have dated exceptions in an overlay outside the version.
  4. Cadences can differ by applicability: unit type, program level or a list of subjects. 5. An academic calendar
  (years and terms) in Settings resolves term-based cadences to dates. 6. When a cycle falls due the scheduler proposes
  it and QA launches it; per-template automatic launch may come later. 7. Cycle templates (annual program report,
  periodic program review, admin unit review, internal audit, custom) carry steps, scope, a configurable review chain and
  a report template. 8. One action register is shared with Accreditations (sources: self-evaluation, assessment result,
  audit finding, accreditor finding). 9. Outcome assessment (PRD §13) lives in Quality Management. 10. KPI values from BI
  are out of scope for now. 11. The AI may draft narratives, suggest actions and summarise for leadership; a person
  approves (DEC-006); evidence files are never sent to the AI (DEC-013).
rationale: >-
  Attaching time to the framework rather than to a menu lets one scheduler serve internal and external obligations, and
  the IQF-to-standard mappings let one collection serve both, so units are not asked twice.
consequences: >-
  New: academic calendar, cadence rules on framework nodes, evidence expectations, subject overlays, cycle templates,
  quality cycles, self-evaluation items, collection tasks, a shared action register, outcome assessment tables. Plan:
  docs/quality-management-plan.md, TASK-077.
