title: The curriculum map is built both ways, top-down and bottom-up, on its own Settings tab; flags, never blocks
status: accepted
context: >-
  The curriculum map was built only from CLO -> PLO links, so a course without CLOs could not be mapped, and the map and
  coverage sat inside each program's Outcomes page. The owner (2026-11-14): move the map and coverage to a stand-alone
  tab between Courses and Learning outcomes, and let a map be built two ways, bottom-up (CLOs -> PLOs) and top-down
  (PLOs -> courses -> CLOs).
decision: >-
  Settings -> Organization has a Curriculum map tab (pick a program; a matrix of courses by study year against the
  program's PLOs and then the institution's ILOs, and a view by PLO). A course's mapping has two sides: its own level
  for each PLO (top-down, "program coverage") and its CLOs' PLOs with their levels (bottom-up). It is edited in three
  places: the map, the PLO card in the program's dialog, and the course's syllabus. Every edit goes into the course's
  syllabus draft (a new draft from the approved version when there is none) and becomes live only when that syllabus is
  approved, like a standards version; the map shows such changes as waiting, and drafts can be submitted from the map at
  once. The course coordinator or a writer of the PLO's program may propose and submit; the chair endorses and the Dean
  approves. How many PLOs one CLO may map to is a syllabus rule (one by default); more is flagged, and the map asks to
  move a CLO instead. Coverage flags a course mapped top-down with no CLO behind it, a top-down level that differs from
  the highest its CLOs reach, a course mapped to no PLO, a course outside the PLO and CLO limits, and a PLO reached at a
  higher level in an earlier year than a lower one. Nothing is blocked except submitting a syllabus that has errors.
rationale: >-
  Programs design their map before CLOs exist; mapping a course first and its CLOs later serves both ways of working
  with one map, and the flags show where the two disagree.
consequences: >-
  A mapping change is live only once the course's whole syllabus can be approved, so a course with an incomplete
  syllabus keeps its proposed mapping waiting until the syllabus is fixed. The live-edit API routes (course-map,
  outcome-links for a CLO) remain for imports and administration but the screens no longer use them for a course's
  mapping; PLO-to-ILO and PLO-to-PEO links stay live program settings. Later: courses that serve no PLO will map to ILOs
  or college or school outcomes (the table already accepts an ILO target); rules for unmapped courses; importing a map
  from Excel waits for a sample workbook.
