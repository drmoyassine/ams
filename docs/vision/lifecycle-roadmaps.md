# lifecycle-roadmaps — Every engagement runs a roadmap to a self-study and on into maintenance

The roadmap always produces a self-study report and continues into maintenance (annual reports, follow-ups, change notices, improvement actions) until the next cycle starts. Leads can adapt the roadmap within guardrails. Success: no engagement is a one-off project; each one closes into the next. PRD §5, §8, §12.
