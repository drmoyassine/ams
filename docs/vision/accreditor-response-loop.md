# accreditor-response-loop — Nothing the accreditor says is lost

Missing items, corrections, queries, findings and confirmations are tracked verbatim across rounds and cycles. Revised submissions are new rounds; submitted content is never edited. Success: any accreditor comment can be traced to its resolution and evidence, and the next self-study's 'response to previous actions' assembles itself. PRD §11.
